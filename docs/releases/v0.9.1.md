# Moebius 0.9.1

Still an early prototype: use it only with throwaway repositories and with model accounts whose spending you watch.

## What changed since 0.9.0

- **Turn the built-in plugins off and on** (#212). The guard, the lens and the localizer have the same switch on the
  Plugins page as the other plugins, and `moebius plugin enable|disable` takes their names; it works at once, without a
  restart, and `moebius.toml` keeps it as `enabled` in `[guard]`, `[lens]` and `[localize]`.
  - The lens off: a run gets no lint and type feedback and no deep check, and goes on as usual.
  - The localizer off: a run starts without the hint of the files its issue likely touches.
  - The guard off: no tool call is checked any more, neither by the built-in safety patterns nor by the rules, so the
    page asks first and the command line needs `--allow-unchecked`. A plugin that decides `tool_call` goes on
    deciding; without one, each call is allowed and its judgement says that the guard was off.
