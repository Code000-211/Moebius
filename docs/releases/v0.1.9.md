# Moebius 0.1.9

Still an early prototype: use it only with throwaway repositories and with model accounts whose spending you watch.

## What changed since 0.1.8

- **A new look: Deep Velvet.** The desktop app is dark now: a violet-black ground with islands of surface, coral for
  the primary button and the chosen item, lavender for runs working now, mint for what runs on its own, soft corners
  and pill tags. Code is highlighted for the dark ground.
- **The chat is one column with turn cards.** A floating bar on top drops down the list of chats and holds the chat's
  title, its agent and place, its status and its actions. Each message of yours heads a card, with what the agent did
  and answered under it. At the foot a composer island holds the field, the pills for agent, place, model and
  reasoning, Terminal and Send.
- **The board has an inspector dock.** Under the columns the card that matters now (the first working one, else the
  first that waits for you) shows its run, guard and state, highlighted on the board. A column names its script on a
  line of its own.
- **An agent's editor in two columns:** its settings on top, the instructions and the Retro's proposals beside its
  skills, MCP servers and extensions.
